Interests

My research sits at the intersection of hardware-aware AI and embodied intelligence. I focus on learning systems that are mathematically grounded yet deployable on robots and edge devices under strict latency, power, and memory constraints—bridging high-level generative modeling with low-level system execution.

Concretely, I’m interested in:

  1. Algorithm–system co-design for latent world models where SoC constraints shape the training objective (e.g., structured sparsity and quantization-aware training for real-time control).
  2. Robustness from incomplete and “dead” industrial data via offline ML and causal reasoning.
  3. Efficient generative 3D reconstruction and motion representations (e.g., dynamics-aware scene models for interpolation and segmentation).

Projects
SAMUEL · paper (accepted to IEEE/WI IAT): arXiv:2507.19991
We present a lightweight latent diffusion model for vocal-conditioned musical accompaniment generation. A soft alignment attention mechanism adaptively combines local and global temporal dependencies across diffusion timesteps, capturing multi-scale musical structure efficiently. Operating in the compressed latent space of a pre-trained VAE, the model achieves a 220× parameter reduction and 52× faster inference while maintaining competitive quality with only 15M parameters—enabling real-time deployment on consumer hardware.
Keywords: latent diffusion, attention, controllable generation, efficient inference

Candidly · project: devpost.com/software/candidly

Inspiration. While doomscrolling I came across “Cluely” (with the slogan “cheat on everything”). We don’t think that’s fair—many candidates work hard to earn roles—and it adds workload and inconsistency for interviewers and senior engineers.

What it does. Candidly is an “Anti‑Cluely” layer for candidates and a Databricks-style platform for interviewers:

  1. Detects and surfaces suspicious gaze and typing patterns during interviews.
  2. Provides a video interviewing tool with templates, coding questions, and an editor for interviewers and candidates.
  3. Records sessions, flags notable moments, and provides HR-facing insights on interviewer performance and improvement directions.
Keywords: interview integrity, gaze/typing signals, video interviews, interviewer analytics
